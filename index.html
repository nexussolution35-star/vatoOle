Who We Are

A world-class joinery workshop with an exclusive portfolio of bespoke craftsmanship.

Vato Style is a specialist joinery and kitchen company serving discerning homeowners and large companies alike. From the first 3D render to the final installed handle, every project is manufactured in-house by qualified artisans who treat your home as their own.

We make bespoke kitchens, built-in cupboards, hardwood doors and frames, wrap doors, counter tops, shopfitting and exclusive custom furniture — all to an uncompromising standard of finish.

How We Work

From concept to completed craftsmanship

A clear, collaborative process that keeps you in control at every stage of your build.

01

Concept

We sit down with you to understand your space, your style and your budget — then capture the brief in detail.

02

Design & 3D Render

Our designers produce true-to-life 3D renders so you can see and refine your kitchen before a single board is cut.

03

Manufacture

Boards are precision cut and edged, doors wrapped, and cabinetry assembled by hand in our own workshop.

04

Execution

Our installation team fits everything to the millimetre on site, leaving you with a flawless, ready-to-use space.
